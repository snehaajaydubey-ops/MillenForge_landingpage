How it began

It started with boredom.A vacation. A thought. A new beginning.

During our 2nd-year college vacation, we had plenty of free time and, honestly, we were getting bored. Instead of letting the vacation pass by, we asked ourselves a simple question: "Why don't we build something of our own?"

That small thought became a bigger conversation.

We started sharing ideas, experimenting with technology, discussing designs, writing code and imagining what we could create together. Five students with different strengths came together with one common goal — to stop waiting for opportunities and start creating them.

And that's how MillenForge was born.

Why the name

What does MillenForge mean?

MILLEN

Represents our generation — young minds growing up with technology, creativity and a desire to build something different.

FORGE

Represents the process of turning raw ideas into something strong and meaningful through effort, experimentation and persistence.

MillenForge = a new generation, forging new ideas into reality.
